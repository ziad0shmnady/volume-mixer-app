# Privacy Policy — Volume Mixer

**Last updated:** August 2026

Volume Mixer does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

## What the app accesses locally

- **Audio devices and streams** — via PipeWire/PulseAudio on your computer, only to show and control volume levels.
- **Settings file** — `~/.config/mixer/settings.json` on your machine (shortcuts, tray, autostart preferences).
- **Autostart entry** — optional `~/.config/autostart/io.github.ziad.Mixer.desktop` when you enable “Open at Startup”.
- **GNOME shortcut** — optional custom keybinding in GNOME Settings when you configure the global mute shortcut.

## What the app does not do

- No analytics or telemetry
- No network requests (except what GTK/GNOME libraries may perform for themes or updates, outside this app’s control)
- No account sign-in
- No cloud sync
